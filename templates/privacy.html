Privacy & Permissions

Clear employee-facing disclosure for photo, location and attendance data.

What this app collects

  • Selfie/photo for Time In and Time Out
  • GPS latitude/longitude and a readable job location at attendance events
  • Attendance actions, breaks, work logs and job notes
  • Device metadata where legally appropriate

What this app does not do by default

It does not continuously track employees in the background. Location capture is limited to Time In, Start Break, End Break and Time Out, unless the company explicitly enables extra job-site tracking.
